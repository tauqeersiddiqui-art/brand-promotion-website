STORE DIRECTORY PROCESS
=======================

Purpose
-------
The /stores page is a help-only directory of online stores. Visitors use our links to
open marketplaces and brand stores and then buy directly on that store's own website.
We do not sell products, take payments, collect order proofs, or process orders,
returns and refunds for purchases made on other stores.

User flow
---------
1. Visitor opens /stores and picks a marketplace or brand.
2. Visitor clicks "Visit store" (or "Search on store") and lands on the store's website.
3. The visitor buys, pays, tracks delivery, and requests returns/refunds on that store.
4. If the visitor needs help, they use that store's own support/help centre.

What the page must say
----------------------
- The page is a directory of links, not a shop or a cashback/refund service.
- Orders, payments, delivery, returns and refunds are handled by the destination store.
- No order proof, refund form, or cashback claim is needed for purchases made elsewhere.
- We never ask for payment, card details, OTPs, or order screenshots for other stores.
- Some links may be affiliate/promotional links; the price the visitor pays is unchanged.

Copy rules
----------
Do not use these phrases on /stores:
- "submit your order proof"
- "claim your cashback"
- "claim after delivery"
- "return window closes"
- "campaign slots"
- "never cancel or return after cashback"

Preferred wording:
- "Pick a store" -> "Buy on the store's site" -> "Use the store's support"
- "Opens the store's own website"
- "There is no order or refund form to fill here"

Implementation notes
--------------------
- File: src/app/stores/page.tsx
- Keep store links pointing to the store's official website and open them in a new tab
  with rel="noopener noreferrer".
- Do not add forms, upload fields, or payment inputs to this page.
- Affiliate/AdSense disclosure stays in src/app/privacy/page.tsx.
- The rest of the site (campaigns, deals, tracking) is separate from this directory.
